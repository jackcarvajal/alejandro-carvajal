Learn Exocad from zero, with a working designer

The scarcest skill in the lab isn't owning the software — it's knowing how to design. Learn Exocad with someone who works real cases every day. Courses run in Spanish; one-on-one mentorship is available in English on request.

Alejandro Carvajal · Dental CAD/CAM designer 4 min read Updated Nov 2026

Buying the scanner and the license is the easy part. What actually defines the quality of your work is knowing how to design — reading a case, respecting margins and contacts, solving an anterior esthetic or a bar over implants. That isn't learned from scattered videos; it's learned by designing, with someone who corrects what you do.

What you'll learn

  • Fundamentals: the Exocad interface, DentalDB, libraries and setup.
  • Crowns & bridges: margins, contacts, morphology and occlusion.
  • Veneers & smile design (DSD): anterior esthetics and mock-up.
  • Implants: screw-retained crowns, custom abutments, emergence profile.
  • Full-arch: hybrid frameworks and bars.
  • Surgical guides and complete workflows.
You learn on real cases, not demo files. The difference between a course and "watching tutorials" is that someone reviews your design and tells you why a margin or contact is wrong — before it reaches a patient.

Courses in Spanish, English mentorship on request

The structured courses are taught in Spanish (live, online for all of Latin America and Spanish speakers in the U.S.). If you prefer English, I offer one-on-one mentorship and case review in English — tell me your level and goals and we'll set it up. Either way, you learn the real daily workflow, not theory.

Who it's for

  • Lab technicians moving into digital design, or leveling up.
  • Dentists who want to bring design in-house.
  • Labs training a team on one consistent workflow.

Prefer not to design and just receive the finished STL? That's the remote design service — this is for those who want to do it themselves.
