Implant design questions

Which implant libraries do you support?

All major libraries in Exocad and 3Shape: Straumann, Nobel Biocare, MIS, Neodent, Dentsply Sirona, Zimmer, BioHorizons, Osstem and more. Send the scan body you used and the implant system, and we match the correct library and Ti-base.

Do you design screw-retained crowns and custom abutments?

Yes. Screw-retained (SCRP) crowns, cement-retained crowns on custom abutments, Ti-base/hybrid abutment design and custom abutment milling files. Emergence profile is shaped for natural soft-tissue esthetics on anterior and posterior cases.

How much does implant crown and abutment design cost?

Implant crown and custom abutment design starts at $15 USD per unit. Full-arch implant hybrids and bars are quoted per case by number of units. No contracts or minimum orders.

What do you need to start an implant case?

An intraoral scan with the scan body in place (STL/PLY), the implant system and connection, and a photo of the emergence/soft tissue if available. For esthetic anterior cases, facial photos help match morphology and shade.

What is the turnaround time?

Single implant crowns and abutments in 24–48 hours. Full-arch implant hybrids typically take 48–72 hours with a 3D preview to approve before final delivery.
