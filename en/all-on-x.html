Full-arch questions

What do you need to design an All-on-X case?

An intraoral or lab scan with scan bodies (or a verified master-model scan), your implant/multi-unit abutment system, and — for immediate cases — the CBCT plus the diagnostic wax-up or denture setup. We design in Exocad or 3Shape with the correct Ti-base and multi-unit libraries.

Do you design screw-retained hybrids and titanium bars?

Yes — monolithic and layered screw-retained hybrids, milled titanium and CoCr bar substructures, and overdenture superstructures, with correct emergence profile, screw-access channels and passive fit.

Which implant systems do you support?

All major systems with open libraries: Nobel Biocare, Straumann, Neodent, MIS, Dentsply, Zimmer, BioHorizons, Osstem and generic multi-unit abutments. If your system isn't in the standard library we source it before starting.

How much does full-arch design cost?

Framework and bar design starts from $25 USD and is quoted per case by number of implants, monolithic vs. layered, and whether an immediate provisional is included. No contracts or minimums.

What is the turnaround?

48–72 business hours for a standard full-arch design, with a preview for approval before final STL delivery. Rush options are available for immediate-load cases.
