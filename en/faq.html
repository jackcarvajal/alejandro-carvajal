Frequently asked questions

How remote dental CAD design works with Alejandro Carvajal from Bogotá, Colombia: what to send, turnaround, revisions, credit plans and payment in USD.

Getting started
Do you work with labs and clinics outside Colombia?
Yes. The design reaches any country by download, with prices in USD and payment by PayPal or Wise. We answer on WhatsApp in English or Spanish. US labs: tell us whether you use Universal (1-32) or FDI tooth numbering.
What do I need to send for my case to start?
A complete case: the prepared arch, the antagonist, the bite registration and the order with tooth number, restoration type and material. If something is missing, the case stays on hold and we tell you exactly what is needed.
Which file formats do you accept?
STL, OBJ, PLY, native exocad files (.constructionfile), 3Shape Communicate projects (.3oxz), exports from iTero, Medit i500/i700 and Primescan, and DICOM files for surgical guide planning.
How do I send my case?
Upload the scan in the online order form, share a Google Drive, WeTransfer or Dropbox link, or send it on WhatsApp at +57 315 983 1800.
Which implant systems do you support?
There are implant libraries for Straumann, Nobel Biocare, Neodent, MegaGen, Osstem, Zimmer, Dentsply Sirona, BioHorizons, Alpha Bio, Cortex and more. If your system is not on the list, get in touch and we can import the library.
Turnaround
How long does a design take?
From 15 minutes for the simplest work (a model, a provisional or a single unit). Every case is different, and conditions and restrictions apply. Rush jobs get priority with a surcharge, subject to availability.
Medium cases (bridges of 4 or more units, implant crowns or abutments): 24 to 48 hours.
Complex cases (full arch, surgical guides, smile design, planning): the timeline is agreed per case.
The clock starts when the case is complete and paid, during business hours: Monday to Saturday, 8 am to 5 pm Colombia time.
Can I send a case at night or on a Sunday?
Yes. Cases are received 24/7, any day. Work starts in business hours, Monday to Saturday from 8 am to 5 pm Colombia time, and turnaround counts from that start. Colombia is on UTC−5 all year, with no daylight saving time: the same as US Eastern in winter and US Central in summer.
Pricing & payment
How much does a design cost?
It depends on the type of restoration and the number of units. The online calculator gives you a quote in about a minute, or send the case and we confirm the exact price. Credit plans lower the price per unit.
How do I pay from outside Colombia?
PayPal (which also takes credit and debit cards) or Wise, in USD. PayPal carries a 7% fee for the platform and currency exchange. Payment confirms the case and the design starts right away; the STL is delivered by download.
What are credit plans?
Prepaid design packs you buy on the platform: Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum. 1 credit = 1 cemented unit (crown, bridge unit, veneer or inlay); an implant crown or abutment is 1.5 credits; full arch, guides and smile design are quoted per case and can also be paid with credits. Credits do not expire, but the balance freezes after 90 days without sending a case (with notices at 60 and 80 days) and reactivates when you buy any plan. Credits are not refundable or transferable.
Do you do a free test case?
No. No service is free, including the first case: every design takes hours of skilled work, software and review.

If you want to know whether your case is feasible before ordering, you can request a case assessment: I review your files and tell you what can be done, how long it takes and what it costs. Its fee depends on the case and the type of service (a crown is not the same as a full arch or a smile design); I confirm it on WhatsApp before starting, and the work starts once payment is confirmed.

Revisions & tracking
How many revisions are included?
You review the design in the 3D viewer on the platform, where you approve it or request changes. Each unit includes 2 revisions, requested within 48 hours of delivery. If you ask for more changes after receiving the second revision, each extra revision costs 20% of the unit price (0.2 credit on plans). Nothing is charged upfront: only if you request it, and I remind you when you ask for the first and second revisions. Corrections due to a designer error are not charged.
How do I track my case?
Enter your order code, which starts with CAD-, at alejandrocadcam.com/seguimiento-caso. With an account, your dashboard shows your cases, the delivery countdown for each one and your credits.
Lab work & Academy
Do you also fabricate restorations?
Yes, veneers for dentists in the United States, finished and shipped. Those orders follow their own terms of service, including shipping and customs.
Do you teach exocad courses?
Yes, through the Alejandro CAD/CAM Academy: online (recorded classes at your own pace plus live sessions), at your lab or in Bogotá, at basic, intermediate and advanced levels, and one-to-one sessions. Courses are taught in Spanish, a good fit for Spanish-speaking technicians in the US. You do not need your own exocad license. Courses open in cohorts with limited seats, and you receive a digital certificate with a code anyone can verify.
