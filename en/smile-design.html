Smile design questions

What do you need for a smile design case?

An intraoral scan (STL/PLY) plus facial and intraoral photos (frontal smile, retracted, lateral). For a full DSD we also use a smile video or a rest/smile photo set. We return a mock-up preview before designing the definitive veneers.

Do you design veneers, mock-ups and full smile makeovers?

Yes. Diagnostic wax-up and mock-up for the trial smile, then layered or monolithic veneer design with final morphology, incisal edge and midline referenced to the face. Ideal for 2–20 unit esthetic cases.

How much does veneer and smile design cost?

Veneer design starts at $12 USD per unit. A full digital smile design (DSD) is quoted per case by number of units and complexity. No contracts or minimum orders.

Which software do you use?

Exocad DentalCAD with the Smile Design module and 3Shape Dental Designer, with photo-based facial referencing for predictable anterior esthetics.

What is the turnaround time?

Single veneers and small cases in 24–48 hours. Full smile makeovers with mock-up approval typically take 48–72 hours including the preview round.
